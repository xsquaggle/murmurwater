# Murmurwater v0.4.0 — Mossglass Fen

The third slice of phase 2: a lamp for the fog, an old road through it, and a second water on the other side.

## What's new
- **The Fog Lamp.** After the first Turning, Pell asks for a lamp. It costs 8 Echoes (satchel, Echoes page), is bought once and kept through every tide. It stands on the skiff's bow and lights itself in the fog.
- **The old sea-road.** North-west of the Drowned Belfry, a double line of iron lights shows in the Murmur Fog once you carry the lamp. Keep between them and the fog lets the skiff through; everywhere else it still turns her back. Each light chimes, far off, as it wakes to the lamp.
- **Mossglass Fen.** A second water as big as Hearthbay: still, shallow and clear over green moss, with reed beds that sway and brush the hull, trees standing in the water, and hanging moss that glows after dark. Six places to find for the chart (Sedge Landing, Glasswater, the Reed Maze, the Standing Trees, the Sump, the Listening Post) and three sea chests.
- **Dr. Ines Calloway** keeps a stilt hut and a field table at Sedge Landing. She measures everything and the numbers will not agree. Six jobs, from A Sense of Scale to The Listening Post; the last gives the second page of Isolde's logbook.
- **Ten new fish**, worth five to seven times their Hearthbay rank. One comes only after dark, one only out of the Sump, one is very old, and one comes only once you have put your ear to the trumpet.
- **Mara's packet box** stands on the landing: it buys your catch at stall prices and has her shelf in the lid, so there is no need to sail home to sell. Once you have reached the Fen her shelf (here and at home) carries the Fenwood Rod, Marsh Worm, Glass Mayfly and the Glass Bead lure.
- **The notice board** on the landing asks for Fen fish while you are in the Fen.
- **The chart** has a page for each water, and "Sail here" carries the skiff between known moorings across the fog.
- **The Mossglass page** of the journal, with a reward from Calloway for filling it.
- **Sound.** Frogs and insects by day and by night, reeds along the hull, the lamp lighting, the chimes of the road, the crossing, the lid and rope of the packet box.
- The Fen's ground is made ready a little at a time while you sail the road, so the change of waters in the thick of the fog does not hitch.

Saves from v0.3 carry over as they are (still save version 4).

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.4.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.4.0-setup.exe` to install it.

## Known issues
- Dr. Calloway is a stand-in figure, not a finished model like Mara, Pell and Wick.
- 90 of the 142 lines are not voiced and show as text: everything Calloway says, Pell's two new jobs, and the 35 lines from v0.3.0. The 52 older lines are voiced as before.
- The Fen has no music of its own yet. Ashore there you hear only the fen; under sail by day the open-water theme plays.
- None of the new sounds has been heard by a person. They were made and levelled by measurement. The lamp's ring and the road's chimes are synthesised and are the first to judge by ear.
- The timings come from the economy simulator, which plays the real rules: the lamp at about 75 minutes, the Fen a few minutes later, Calloway's last job at about two and a half hours, the Mossglass page soon after, the Listener at about four hours for a keeper who sits up for it. No person has played this slice end to end yet.
- Frame times on the RTX 3060 target are still unmeasured: F3 shows them. The Fen draws about 0.76 million triangles a frame on Medium against Hearthbay's 0.44 million.
- Where the Sump's deep water meets the shallows the edge shows as coarse facets.
