# Murmurwater v0.1.1 — casts go where you look

A small fix on top of v0.1.0.

- **Casts no longer drift left.** The camera used to sit beside you but look back at you, so the view pointed about 10 degrees to the right of the way you were facing and every throw seemed to veer left. The camera now looks the way you face: the landing ring sits in the middle of the screen and the lure flies straight up the view. You stand a little right of centre, which also keeps the water ahead of you clear.

Everything else is as in v0.1.0. Saves carry over.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.1.1-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.1.1-setup.exe` to install it.
