# Murmurwater v0.3.0 — Lantern Pots and the Drowned Bell

The second slice of phase 2: gear that changes how you fish, pots that fish while you do, and a bell that turns the tide.

## What's new
- **Mara's shelf has four pages.** Rods can be tuned, ten steps each. Every bait has a ground it is best on. Lures ride the line above the bait and never wear out. A better creel holds more and keeps fish worth more. A cap, a coat, boots and a charm each do one small thing.
- **Satchel, Gear page.** What all of it adds up to, in one place, and you can change rod, bait, lure and clothes from anywhere.
- **Lantern Pots.** Mara gives you the first one for a job. Stand facing open water, or lie still in the skiff, and press F to set it; F again hauls it, and holding F takes an empty pot up to move it. A pot traps fish that are already in your journal, about one every 50 seconds, holds three and stops. It fishes only while the game is running. Full pots show on the HUD, the compass and the chart.
- **Four new jobs.** Lanterns in the Water and A Full Stall from Mara, then The Drowned Bell and The Turning from Pell.
- **The Drowned Belfry.** A church tower standing in the sea north-west of Gull Point, with its bell still hung and a sea chest on its shoal.
- **The Bell.** Ringing it turns the tide: your coins go back to the sea, and with them the tuning, creel, bait and pot upgrades they paid for (rods, lures, clothes, pots and the boat stay), you wake on the Hearthbay dock at first light, and Echoes come back for everything you have ever earned. Before you pull the rope, a page says exactly what the tide takes, what you keep and how many Echoes it will give. Waiting never loses any.
- **Echoes** buy ten lasting upgrades (satchel, Echoes page): fish sell for more, a harder reel, a steadier line, more pots, coins to start each tide with, and others.
- **The keeper's own things.** Four pieces of clothing that are found, not bought. Worn together they do more.
- After a Turning, people very nearly remember you.
- **Sound.** The bell tolls like a church bell, pots splash in and come up dripping, the tide turns with two long waves.
- A new game begins at first light.

Saves from v0.2 carry over (save version 4).

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.3.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.3.0-setup.exe` to install it.

## Known issues
- The 35 new lines (the four jobs, and what people say after a Turning) are not voiced yet; they show as text. The 52 older lines are voiced as before.
- The new sounds (the bell's toll, pots going in and coming up, the tide turning, buying an Echo) were levelled by measurement, not by ear.
- The timings (the skiff at about 23 minutes, pots at 42, the first ring at 75) come from the economy simulator, which plays the real rules. No person has played this slice end to end yet.
- Only Hearthbay Waters is in. The fog lamp that opens the next region will cost Echoes and is not for sale yet, so Echoes have ten upgrades to go on and nothing else.
- Frame times on the RTX 3060 target are still unmeasured: F3 shows them.
