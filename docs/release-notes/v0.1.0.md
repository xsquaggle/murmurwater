# Murmurwater v0.1.0 — Hearthbay vertical slice

The first playable story, in Hearthbay: from your first three fish to relighting the Gull Point lamp.

## What's in it
- **The Hearthbay story.** Six quests from Mara, Wick and Pell. Mara teaches you the dock and buys your catch, Wick needs a fish he drew before he saw it, and Pell rows you out to the dark lighthouse on Gull Point.
- **Characters.** Generated, animated models for you, Mara, Pell, Wick and Biscuit the harbour cat. NPCs turn to face you, wave, talk with their hands, and Wick sits on an upturned tackle box with his sketchbook until you come over.
- **Fishing feel.** Authored rod animation with a rod that bends and swings against the fish's runs, your hands on the grip and reel, and a proper show-off when you land one.
- **Harbour life.** Mara's stall and shop (rods and bait), a notice board with odd jobs that refresh while you play, a satchel with your catch, gear, journal and quests, and relics that bring luck.
- **Voices.** Every line is voiced: Mara, Pell and Wick each have their own voice (subtitles can be turned off in Settings).
- **Settings.** Window mode and resolution, graphics quality, five volume sliders, mouse sensitivity and invert, UI scale, camera shake, subtitles, and key rebinding.
- **F3** shows a performance overlay (frame rate, frame times, draw calls).
- Saves carry over from v0.0.1.

## Temporary (will be replaced)
- Sound effects are simple synth sounds; no music yet.
- No chart yet (M just tells you so); boats and sailing come in the next phase.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.1.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.1.0-setup.exe` to install it with Start-menu and desktop shortcuts.
