# Murmurwater v0.1.0 — Hearthbay vertical slice

The first playable story, in Hearthbay: from your first three fish to relighting the Gull Point lamp.

## What's in it
- **The Hearthbay story.** Six quests from Mara, Wick and Pell. Mara teaches you the dock and buys your catch, Wick needs a fish he drew before he saw it, and Pell rows you out to the dark lighthouse on Gull Point. Every quest fish can be caught from the end of the dock (the Lampjaw only from dusk to dawn).
- **Casting that feels thrown.** A two-handed wind-up, a rod that loads and whips, the lure slung off the tip on a real arc with the line paying out behind it, a splash sized to the throw, and a camera that kicks. A ring on the water shows where the cast will land; release in the bright band for a clean cast.
- **Fish shadows.** Shapes roam under the surface near you. With a line out, one circles in on the float, darts at it for each nibble and takes it for the bite. A fighting fish drags its shadow with it and scatters the others when it thrashes.
- **Recorded sound and music.** The swish of the rod and the reel paying out, plops, bites, splashes and a creaking line; footsteps for wood, cobble, grass and sand; gulls by day, lapping waves, a dock that creaks and a cat that purrs. Three themes (Hearthbay by day, Hearthbay by night, Gull Point) that crossfade, and a sting for a rare catch.
- **Voices.** Every line is voiced: Mara, Pell and Wick each have their own voice (subtitles can be turned off in Settings). Music dips under speech.
- **Characters.** Generated, animated models for you, Mara, Pell, Wick and Biscuit the harbour cat. NPCs turn to face you, wave and talk with their hands; conversations are framed from the side so you can see who you are talking to.
- **Harbour life.** Mara's stall and shop (rods and bait), a notice board with odd jobs that refresh while you play (a taken job can be given up), a satchel with your catch, gear, journal and quests, and relics that bring luck. Houses, the stall, crates and people are solid.
- **The lighthouse.** Once the lamp is lit it throws light over the point and sweeps a slow double beam you can see from the harbour. At night you carry a small lantern glow so the ground near you stays readable.
- **Settings.** Window mode and resolution, graphics quality, five volume sliders, mouse sensitivity and invert, UI scale, camera shake, subtitles, and key rebinding.
- **F3** shows a performance overlay (frame rate, frame times, draw calls for the whole frame).
- **Saves** carry over from v0.0.1. A save that cannot be read is kept aside instead of being overwritten, and starting the game twice brings the first window forward.

## Known issues
- The Hollow Lantern lives out at the Teeth and needs a boat, so the Hearthbay journal tops out at 8 of 9 for now.
- No chart yet (M just tells you so); boats and sailing come in the next phase.
- The music and a few layered sounds (line snap, bite, hook set) were chosen by measurement, not by ear; feedback on anything that grates is welcome.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.1.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.1.0-setup.exe` to install it with Start-menu and desktop shortcuts.
