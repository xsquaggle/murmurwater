# Murmurwater v0.0.1 — pipeline prototype

**This is an early prototype, not the vertical slice.** It exists to prove the Windows build pipeline and to let you feel the first version of the fishing.

## What's in it
- Hearthbay at golden hour with a live day/night cycle: water, sky, clouds, lighthouse, village, dock.
- Fishing on the dock: hold left mouse to charge a cast (release in the bright band for a clean cast), click when the bobber goes under, then hold left mouse to reel and A/D to steer against the fish's runs. Let go when the tension bar peaks or the line snaps.
- Nine Hearthbay fish, selling at the foot of the dock (E), saving and loading.

## Temporary (will be replaced)
- The player and fish models are simple stand-ins until the generated models arrive.
- Sound effects are simple synth sounds; no music or voices yet.
- No menus or settings screen yet.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.0.1-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info" → "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.0.1-setup.exe` to install it with a Start-menu and desktop shortcut.
