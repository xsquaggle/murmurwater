# Murmurwater v0.6.0 — Things Fished Up

The fifth slice of phase 2: not everything on the bottom is a fish. Crates, sea chests and relics now come up on the line, and the relics have a shelf of their own.

## What's new
- **Finds.** Once you have landed a dozen fish, a cast now and then comes down over something that is not a fish. Nothing nibbles and no shadow comes: the float is drawn slowly under, and you have a couple of seconds to strike. Miss it and it is still there, and draws the float under again in a few seconds. A find uses no bait. What lies under a cast stays there until the cast is used (a fish hooked, or the thing brought up), so casting and reeling straight back in does not hurry the next find along, and neither does loading the game again.
- **The haul.** What you have struck is a dead weight. Hold the reel to wind it up a little at a time. When it knocks on the bottom (a thud, a gold ring on the water, and the panel says so), let go for a moment and it slides over; reel on and it catches fast: the line strains several times as quickly and nothing comes in until you leave the reel alone. Resting costs only a little line, as it settles back slowly. Break the line and a crate or chest is gone; a relic stays where it lay.
- **Drift crates** come up in any water: coins (never less than a good hand would have had from the fish of that cast, on your tackle and at your Rhythm) and a handful of bait. **Sea chests** come up only over the Teeth in Hearthbay and among the Standing Trees and in the Sump in the Fen: more coins, a Moonpearl, and a piece of gear sold nowhere in the first chest you bring up in each region (in Hearthbay the Mirror Spoon, a lure for stranger fish; in the Fen the Dowser's Bob, a charm that brings finds to the line a little more often).
- **Eight new relics, fished up.** Each lies in its own water (one of them in either of two), some only at certain hours, and none comes up before somebody has spoken of it: a rumour arrives as a message when its time in the story comes, and the relic is in the water from then. In the right water at the right hour a relic comes up in about three casts and never takes more than eleven. Each has a small gift (bites sooner, pots fill faster, a longer bite window and so on) and a few lines about whose it was.
- **The Keeper's Shelf.** A new page in the satchel (Tab), there once you own a relic or have heard of one, with a place for every relic of the waters you have reached: the ones you have, the ones you have heard of with what was said, and empty places for the rest. Beside each relic are two hollows. A **Moonpearl** set in a hollow adds half that relic's gift again, two double it, and a pearl can be lifted and set beside another relic at any time for nothing. Pearls you already had are free to set.
- **Three tester's starts.** F8 on the title screen now steps through three: this slice's first, then hands aboard, then Mossglass Fen, then your own game again.
- **Smaller things.** Long messages wrap instead of running across the screen. The prompts along the bottom hide while a catch is held up.

Saves from v0.5 carry over (the save is now version 6 and older ones are brought up to it when loaded). An older save is told, a moment after you click to begin, every rumour its keeper has already earned.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.6.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.6.0-setup.exe` to install it.

## To see this slice without playing up to it
Press **F8** on the title screen. The game comes back as a keeper the simulator played for nearly four hours: nightfall, the end of the home dock, the Puffin afloat, four relics and three Moonpearls never set. The rumours of both waters arrive a moment after you click to begin. In about ten minutes you can: open the satchel (Tab) to the Shelf, read what is said, and set a pearl beside a relic; cast straight out from the dock's end into Lantern Cove, where the Keeper's Spectacles lie at night, and haul them up when the float is drawn under (a few casts, with fish between); keep fishing for a crate, which comes about once in a dozen fish. A chest takes longer: sail the Puffin to the Teeth, the rocks to the south-west (the chart, M, shows them), where about one find in three is a chest (the Cork Float With Two Names lies there too, and usually comes up first). Press F8 again for the older starts, and once more for your own game. Nothing done in a tester's start is saved.

## Known issues
- Nobody has heard the ten new sounds in play (the float drawn under, the knock, the snag, coming free, the box breaking the surface, a crate prised open, a relic, a rumour, a pearl set and lifted): they were made without credits from free recordings and set by measurement. The knock is the one that matters: please say if it is hard to hear over the reel, and whether the slow gulp of a find is plainly not a bite.
- Nobody has hauled a find by hand. Whether the knock leaves enough time to let go, and whether the haul is still a pleasure the tenth time, are the two things most worth an opinion.
- A find's coins go by your tackle and your Rhythm, so late in the game a crate's card can show thousands. It is meant: a crate is never worth less than a good hand would have had from that cast.
- The relics held up are small shapes built in code, and a held crate or chest covers the keeper's hands. Looked at only in the software renderer the tests use.
- Timings are the simulator's, for a keeper who knows where every fish bites: the first relic at about 15 minutes for one who has done Wick's drawing job by the first night (about 40 otherwise), a find every seven to nine minutes for that keeper (every four or so for somebody who fishes one spot without a pause), about a tenth of all coins from finds, eleven or twelve of the twelve relics in seven hours. Earlier milestones moved by a few minutes (Biscuit at about 60, the Fen at about 85, the Puffin afloat at about 212).
- 121 of the 173 lines are still not voiced, and the Fen still has no music of its own. Both wait on credits.
- Everything listed under v0.5.0 that is not mentioned here still stands.
