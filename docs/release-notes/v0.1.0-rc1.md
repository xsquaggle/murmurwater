# Murmurwater v0.1.0-rc1 — preview build

A preview of v0.1.0 (the Hearthbay vertical slice) to try two new things before the full release:

- **Casting that feels thrown.** A two-handed wind-up, a rod that loads and whips, the lure slung off the tip on a real arc with the line paying out behind it, a splash sized to the throw, and a camera and sound that react. A ring on the water shows where the cast will land.
- **Fish shadows.** Shapes roam under the surface near you. With a line out, one circles in on the float, darts at it for each nibble and takes it for the bite.

## Known issues in this preview
- The Lampjaw (Pell's quest) cannot be caught from the Hearthbay shore yet; fixed in v0.1.0.
- Sound effects are still simple synth sounds; no music yet.

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.1.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway").
