# Murmurwater v0.5.0 — Hands Aboard

The fourth slice of phase 2: a crew of two who fish from your boat while you do, and a second, bigger boat to carry them.

## What's new
- **Deckhands.** Hands who sign on sit in a berth aboard your boat and fish for you. They fish only while the game is open and the boat lies still, they can land only fish your journal already knows from the water under the keel, and what they land goes in a crew basket of its own (8 fish in the skiff, 20 in the Puffin) that sells with your catch at Mara's stall or her packet box. A full basket stops them until it is sold. The HUD has a pill for the basket and the satchel has a Crew page that says what each hand is doing and what they have earned.
- **Biscuit**, the dock cat, signs on through Wick's job "Whose Cat" (three fish off the Hearthbay dock, once the Lantern Pots are open). She hooks a small fish out with a paw every half minute and eats every fourth: those are her wages. With your own line out she leans to the water and her tail flicks about a second and a half before a bite.
- **The Puffin.** Dr. Calloway points out a sloop lying in the reeds west of her stake. Look her over from the skiff, and Pell at Hearthbay knows whose she was. For 40,000 coins he floats her off and fits her out: a gaff sloop with tanbark sails, about a third faster than the skiff under sail, slower to turn, deeper in the water, with two berths and the big crew basket.
- **Pell's boatyard.** Once the Puffin is found, Pell at the home dock will talk about the boats. He keeps whichever boat you are not sailing and brings the other round when asked. The crew move with you: in the skiff there is one berth, and whoever has none waits ashore.
- **Mister Hollis.** A gentleman made of fog stands on the water by the eighth light of the old sea-road with a carpet bag, waiting for the packet. He wants a berth of his own, so he comes aboard once the Puffin is yours: sail up to him, stop, and hail him. He fishes a hand line every three quarters of a minute, lands better fish than the cat, and eats none, but only after dark or where the fog lies. By daylight he thins to an outline and waits.
- **Two tester's starts.** F8 on the title screen now steps through two: this slice's first, then Mossglass Fen's from v0.4.0, then your own game again (see below).
- **Smaller things.** The compass moves down a row when the pills along the top reach it. A resting hull's ring on the water follows her shape. The Hold page says what the pots and the crew have aboard above the grid instead of below it. The wreck's reeds stand back from her so she can be seen.

Saves from v0.4 carry over (the save is now version 5 and older ones are brought up to it when loaded).

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.5.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.5.0-setup.exe` to install it.

## To see this slice without playing up to it
Press **F8** on the title screen. The game comes back as a keeper the simulator played for nearly four hours by the real rules: Biscuit signed on, the wreck of the Puffin found, the price of her refit in the purse, and Mister Hollis waiting on the road. In about ten minutes you can: board the skiff and lie still off the dock to watch the cat work; land, ask Pell "About the Puffin." and pay for her; sail the Puffin north-west past the Drowned Belfry (the chart, M, carries her to the belfry) and down the sea-road; stop beside the gentleman who stands on the water to the left of the road, a little past half way, and hail him; then lie still in the fog, or anywhere after dark, and watch him fish. Press F8 again for the Mossglass Fen start from v0.4.0, and once more for your own game. Nothing done in a tester's start is saved.

## Known issues
- The crew's sounds (the cat's catch and her meal, Mister Hollis's line, his thinning and return, the full basket, the boatyard, the sloop's sail) are stand-in tones until the recordings are delivered.
- 121 of the 173 lines are not voiced and show as text, including everything Mister Hollis says and the new lines of Pell, Wick and Calloway. The 52 older lines are voiced as before.
- Timings are the simulator's, for a keeper who knows where every fish bites: Biscuit at about 50 minutes, the Puffin found at about 105, afloat at about 220, Mister Hollis a few minutes later. The crew bring in about a twentieth of all coins. No person has played this slice.
- From the helm, sailing straight at Mister Hollis puts the Puffin's mast in front of him.
- Checked only in the software renderer the tests use: how Mister Hollis looks thinned by daylight (he is drawn as a pattern of dots, finer at higher resolutions), and frame times with the Puffin and two hands aboard. F3 shows frame times.
- Everything listed under v0.4.0 that is not mentioned here still stands.
