# Murmurwater v0.2.0 — the Keeper's Skiff

The first slice of phase 2: a boat of your own and the water to sail it on.

## What's new
- **Your own boat.** When the lamp is relit, Pell gives you the Keeper's Skiff. Walk up to her and press E to climb in, and E again to step ashore anywhere there is ground beside her.
- **Free sailing.** W fills the sail, S checks her and backs water, A and D put the tiller over. She carries her way and glides, heels into turns, throws a wake and spray, and stops afloat when she touches the shallows. You sit at the tiller; a compass strip along the top shows your heading, your speed, and which way your goal and the moorings lie.
- **Fishing from the boat.** Let her settle, then cast as you do ashore: you stand up amidships to fish. The deep water at the Teeth holds fish the dock never sees, the Hollow Lantern among them.
- **A chart (M).** Places go on the chart, and pay a little, the first time you sail close to them. Lying at a mooring you can pick another known mooring on the chart and be carried there; the passage takes some of the day.
- **Two new story jobs, fully voiced.** Pell sends you out to Kettle Isle for the keeper's kettle (a new relic), and Mara wants a Twofold Flounder from the Teeth.
- **Something buried.** There is a sandbar the chart does not name yet, with a sea chest in it.
- **The Murmur Fog.** Hearthbay Waters end in a fog that hums, closes in around the skiff and turns her back. It will take a fog lamp to go further.
- **Sound.** A sailing theme on open water by day, a sting for new places, and the skiff's own sounds: hull wash, sail, rope, boarding, bumps.
- Pell no longer rows you about once you have a boat; he goes home to the Hearthbay dock.
- The camera comes in closer beside cliffs and sea stacks instead of climbing over them.

Saves from v0.1 carry over (save version 3).

## How to run
Download `Murmurwater-0.2.0-portable.exe` and double-click it (Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway"), or run `Murmurwater-0.2.0-setup.exe` to install it.

## Known issues
- The 12 new voice lines (Pell's kettle errand, Mara's flounder) were checked by transcript and loudness, not by ear.
- The skiff's sounds and the sailing theme were levelled by measurement, not by ear.
- You can only fast travel from a mooring, and only to places already on the chart.
- The coins the new places, the chest and the two jobs pay have not been through an economy simulation yet; that is the next piece of work.
- The Twofold Flounder now bites only at the Teeth.
- Frame times on the RTX 3060 target are still unmeasured: F3 shows them.
