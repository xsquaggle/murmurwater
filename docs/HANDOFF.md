# Handoff

Last updated: 2026-11-23, phase 2 fifth slice (things fished up: crates, sea chests and relics on the line, the haul, the Keeper's Shelf), released as v0.6.0. What is left of phase 2 is the voices and music that wait on credits; phase 3 (Saltbone Reach) is next.

## Start here (new session)
1. Read `docs/DIRECTIVE.md` (the brief, verbatim) and this file.
2. Skim `docs/ROADMAP.md` for the phase checklist and `docs/DECISIONS.md` for why things are the way they are.
3. `npm ci && npm test && npm run typecheck`, then `npx vite build && npx vite preview --port 4173` and run the bots (below). `xvfb-run -a node e2e/electron-smoke.cjs` checks the desktop app loads every model, voice, sound and music file.

## Where things stand
- Hearthbay plays end to end: Mara's first jobs, selling at her stall, the shop and notice board, Wick's sketch, Pell's ferry to Gull Point, relighting the lamp, and back. Every quest fish is catchable from the dock.
- The cast is built to feel thrown (wind-up, whip, lure flight on an arc, line paying out, splash by power, camera kick, aim ring); fish shadows roam and one stalks the float before each bite.
- Generated models are in for the player, NPCs, Biscuit the cat, fish, skiff and harbour props (see `docs/ASSETS.md`); stand-ins remain only where a file is missing.
- Sound: 118 recorded effect files, 4 looping themes and 3 stings are wired in (`src/audio/audio.ts`); each one-shot keeps a synth stand-in for a missing file. All 52 voice lines are recorded.
- Sailing (v0.2.0): Pell gives the Keeper's Skiff when the lamp is relit. The player boards it from any shore, sails it freely inside Hearthbay Waters (Kettle Isle, the Teeth, Pip's Bar), fishes from it when it is still, finds places for the chart, travels between known moorings from the chart, and is turned back by the Murmur Fog at the edge. Two more story quests (hb-07 the kettle, hb-08 the Teeth flounder) use it; a sea chest waits on Pip's Bar.
- Economy and gear (v0.3.0): Mara's shelf has four pages (rods and bait with rod tuning, lures and creel, clothes, Lantern Pots); the satchel has a Gear page that totals everything and an Echoes page once the Bell is known. Hearthbay is tuned by the simulator: the skiff at about 23 minutes, pots at 43, the first ring of the Bell between 73 and 79.
- Lantern Pots (v0.3.0): set and hauled with F from shore, dock or the skiff; they trap journal fish on play time only, hold three and stop.
- The Drowned Bell (v0.3.0): the belfry stands on a shoal north-west of Gull Point. Pell's hb-09 finds it, hb-10 rings it; ringing turns the tide (coins and what they bought are taken, Echoes are paid on lifetime coins, the keeper wakes on the dock). Four more quests in all: hb-p1-pots, hb-08b-stall, hb-09-bell, hb-10-turning.
- Mossglass Fen (v0.4.0): the second region, north-west across the Murmur Fog. Pell's hb-11 asks for the Fog Lamp (8 Echoes, satchel), hb-12 sends the keeper down the old sea-road past the belfry. At Sedge Landing: Dr. Calloway (six jobs, fen-01 to fen-06), Mara's packet box (sells and has her shelf), a notice board that asks for Fen fish. Ten fish, the Fenwood Rod, two baits, a lure, two wearables, two relics, three chests, six chart places, the Mossglass page of the journal. In the simulator: the lamp at about 75 minutes, the Fen at 80, Calloway's last job at 150, the page at 165, the Listener at about 230 for a keeper who sits up for it. The simulator's keeper knows where and when every fish bites; a person learns it from the journal's hints, so read these as the fastest sensible play, not the usual one. `tests/sim.test.ts` holds only that at least three keepers in five land the Listener inside seven hours.
- Hands aboard (v0.5.0): deckhands sit in the boat's berths and fish while she lies still and the game runs, into a crew basket (skiff 8, Puffin 20) that sells with the catch and stops them when it is full. Biscuit signs on through Wick's `hb-s1-cat` (a fish every 30 s, small ones, eats every fourth, leans to the water before a bite). Calloway's `fen-03b-wreck` sends the keeper to look over the wreck of the Puffin from the skiff and is handed in to Pell, which opens his boatyard (flag `boatyard-open`); `hb-13-refit` is the 40,000 coins. The Puffin is faster, deeper, slower to turn, with two berths. Pell at the home dock swaps the boats. Mister Hollis (`fen-s1-gentleman`, from Calloway after `fen-04`) stands on the water by the sea-road and is hailed from a boat with a berth of his own, which is the Puffin; he fishes every 45 s, better fish, only at night or in fog, and by day is drawn as an outline. In the simulator: Biscuit at about 50 minutes, the Puffin found at 106, afloat at about 220, Hollis a few minutes later; the crew bring 3 to 4% of all coins.
- Things fished up (v0.6.0): once a dozen fish have been landed, a cast now and then comes down over a drift crate, a sea chest (only over the Teeth, the Standing Trees and the Sump) or a relic somebody has spoken of. Nothing nibbles; the float is drawn slowly under; the strike takes a dead weight that is hauled by pumping the reel and letting go when it knocks on the bottom. A crate holds coins and bait, a chest more coins, a Moonpearl and, the first of each water, a piece of gear sold nowhere (the Mirror Spoon, the Dowser's Bob). Eight new relics lie in a water at an hour after a point in the story, each with a rumour told once. The satchel has a Shelf page: a place for every relic of the waters reached, and two hollows beside each where a Moonpearl adds half the relic's gift again. A cast counts toward the next find only when it is used (a fish hooked, the find aboard, a crate or chest gone with the line), so casting and reeling in gains nothing. In the simulator: a find every seven to nine minutes of fishing for about a tenth of all coins, the first relic at about 15 minutes for a keeper who has done Wick's drawing job by the first night (about 40 otherwise), 11 or 12 of 12 relics in seven hours. The milestones moved a little: Biscuit about 60, the Fen about 85, the Puffin found about 107 and afloat about 212, the Mossglass page about 200, the Listener about 250.
- Settings are separate from the save (Electron `userData/settings.json`, browser localStorage). An unreadable save is kept aside, never overwritten: both files when neither loads, the main one when the game goes on from the backup.
- The tester's starts: F8 on the title screen reloads the game with `?tester` (the newest slice: `src/data/tester/finds.json`), F8 again with `?tester=crew` (`crew.json`, v0.5.0's), again with `?tester=fen` (`fen.json`, v0.4.0's), and once more the player's own game. A tester's start has a save store that keeps nothing. Each file is a save the simulator's keeper played to the doorstep of that slice (`npm run tester-save`, `tools/tester-save.ts`, one function a start); `tests/tester.test.ts` fails when the rules have moved on from one, and then the files are simply made again. When a slice is added, add a start to `TESTER_STARTS` there and to `TESTERS` in `src/main.ts`, newest first. Flags the game sets outside `src/core` (`met-<npc>`, `kettle-taken`) are added there by hand: a new one of that kind must be too.

## Code map
- `src/core`: pure logic (fishing, economy, quests, saves, world heightfield, dock, `boat.ts` handling, `seaways.ts` route finding, `landing.ts` where a boat floats and a person can step off, `gear.ts` the one fold of every bonus into `stats()`, `pots.ts`, `bell.ts`, `content.ts` with the regions, sea-roads and `regionAhead`, `flora.ts` where trees stand in the water, `crew.ts` the deckhands' rules, `finds.ts` what a cast comes down over and what is in it, `relics.ts` the Shelf, rumours and pearls). Tested in Node.
- `src/data`: JSON content (fish, rods, baits, boats, relics, finds, quests, NPCs, regions with places, NPC spots, critters, ferry points, chart places, moorings, chests).
- `src/game`: glue (game loop, camera, input, town: NPCs, talk, screens, cutscenes; `sailing.ts`: the player's boat, boarding, the helm, discovery, fast travel; `potfield.ts`: pots in the world; `hands.ts`: the crew's clocks, what they look like at work and what is said of it; `trade.ts`: every buy, tune, wear and Echo button; settings).
- `tools/sim`: the economy simulator (`npm run sim`).
- `src/render`: three.js (world scene, terrain, water with fish shadows, sky, avatar, fishing view: lure, line, float, splashes; `boat-rig.ts`: hull, sail, wake, spray, tiller, the Fog Lamp, where the person sits and stands, the berths; `crew-figures.ts`: the cat and Mister Hollis aboard; `find-models.ts`: crate, chest, the oilcloth bundle and the relics, on the line and held up; NPC figures, cat, fish models, assets; `looks.ts`: a region's colours; `sea-road.ts`: the lights in the fog; `fen-scene.ts`: reeds, the grove, lilies, night lights; `fen-props.ts`: data-driven props with stand-ins; `region-prep.ts`: the next region's ground made ahead of the crossing).
- `src/audio`: mixer, sound bank, music.
- `src/ui`: DOM HUD (with the helm compass), dialogue and screens (`chart.ts` paints the chart).
- `e2e`: the bots.

## Sailing, for whoever touches it next
- Conventions: forward is (sin h, cos h); a positive heading change is a turn to port; rudder +1 is the left key. North is −z, east is +x. The chart is north-up.
- `core/boat.ts` never lets the hull's sample points sit in less water than the draft: it slides along the shore, and when a turn is blocked it works the hull toward deeper water. `tests/boat.test.ts` holds it to that.
- A boat's place is saved in `boats.at`, whether the player is in it in `world.onBoat`, found places in `world.charted`. A save made in the middle of the step between boat and shore is written as already arrived.
- Landing and boarding share one reach, measured from the hull (`core/landing.ts`): a landing is never offered further off than `LANDING.reach`, and boarding reaches `LANDING.slack` further, so the player can always get back aboard from where she put them ashore. Before this rule a reviewer found shores on Kettle Isle and Hearthbay where the player was stranded for good. `tests/content.test.ts` drives the skiff at every island from all round to hold it.
- Content rules are tests: every boat must float at every mooring with somewhere to step off, reach every mooring, chart place and quest ground from the home dock, and every chest must be landable (`tests/content.test.ts`).
- Bots drive the boat with `window.bot.sailTo(x, z)` from `e2e/sail-helpers.cjs`, which follows `findRoute` with the helm keys. Installing those helpers also grants the sail bot's starting state and puts the player on Gull Point, unless called with `{ grant: false }`. `e2e/fog-helpers.cjs` adds `window.fog` (the road pilot, the chart's buttons, talking). Set `g.skipRender = true` in long loops.
- The wake is foam in the water shader (`water.ts wakeFoam`) fed by the track `boat-rig.ts Wake` records; there is no wake mesh. The Murmur Fog's look is `WorldScene.murk` (scene fog plus the sky's `uMurk`).
- The sail rig has two morph targets, `belly` (0) and `furl` (1). `Sailing.restT` decides when the sail is down: nobody aboard, or still for 1.2 s with no call for way. It is the player's sign that she has settled and a cast will be allowed.
- Sea chests are data (`regions.json chests`: place, coins, baits, a line of text); `town.ts openChest` pays them once (flag `chest:<id>`), and `world-scene.ts` swaps the shut model for the open one.
- `Heightfield.shapeAt` and `landing.ts solid` run hundreds of times a frame at sea: keep them free of allocation.

## Regions and the Fen, for whoever touches them next
- One coordinate space for the whole sea. A region is a disc (`centre`, `radius` 700) in `regions.json`; its last 110 m is fog. A sea-road (`ROADS` in `core/content.ts`: ends `a` and `b`, `halfWidth`, the two regions) is the only way through, and only with the Fog Lamp. `tests/roads.test.ts` holds that the road joins quiet water to quiet water, that the murmur lifts only down its middle, that the region changes half way along and only on the road (with a dead band, `SWAP_BAND`, so a boat on the line does not flip back and forth), that the road's water is open water with only common and uncommon fish, and that a boat with the lamp gets across while one without is turned back.
- Only the active region is built. `Game.enterRegion(id)` tears down the old `WorldScene` and builds the new one in the same three.js scene; `arriveIn(state, id)` in `core/quests.ts` is the save's side of it (region, discovered, the `reached:<id>` flag, and `localiseBoard`: the board's untaken jobs read again for the new water with the same serial and timer, never a fresh deal, or crossing would be a free re-roll). The swap happens at half way along the road, or under the fade of a chart journey.
- The pause at the swap is short because the ground is already made: `Game.step` asks `regionAhead` (with the boat's heading) while the skiff is on a road and `RegionPrep.pump` builds the terrain a few milliseconds a frame (`terrainSteps`, a generator whose longest turn is about 3 ms at the finest ground: keep it so, `pump` cannot split a turn). `Game.lastSwap` records what the swap cost; the fog bot holds `readyBefore === 1`. Anything new that is slow to build for a region should be given to `RegionPrep` the same way, not built in the `WorldScene` constructor.
- The ground is two meshes: the land (casts shadows) and the bed below the waterline as its child (does not), with flat bed drawn as coarse fans. `tests/terrain.test.ts` checks every face looks up and no edge is left open. The lattice is jittered, so a cell picks whichever diagonal does not make a sliver.
- Every point of a region is in a zone: `zoneAt` falls back to the region's open zone (`openZone`), and a new region must have one. Null used to mean "every zone", which put every Fen fish on the sea-road. Each zone has a `hint` ("in the Sump") that the journal uses to say where a fish bites (`fishHint`).
- Whatever leaves the scene for good at a swap must be freed: `WorldScene.dispose` does its own root, `Town.enterRegion` the people and cats, `Sailing.enterRegion` the spare ferry boat, all through `assets.ts freeOwn`.
- The goal line (`currentGoal`) prefers work in the water the keeper is in; a job across the fog shows only when nothing can be done here. Keep that order when adding a region.
- Goods, places and props are data. `from: "reached:<region>"` on a rod, bait or lure puts it on every shelf once that region is reached. A look-at place (`places` with `prompt`, `text`, `flag`, `dark`) is the stake and the ear trumpet. A region's `props` name a model in `fen-props.ts SPECS`, which has a stand-in for each; `block` gives it a solid circle. An NPC with `thing: true` is not a person (the packet box): it has no voice, its one line is read the first time only, and it has its own sound.
- A quest can be handed in to someone other than its giver (`receiver`); `handsInTo(q)` is who.
- Reeds and other thin leaves use `fen-scene.ts swaying()`: lit mostly as if facing the sky, plus light through the leaf toward a low sun (taken from the sun's strength more than its hue, or dusk turns them crimson).
- The water shader has a float `uTurbid` (clearness); `uMurk` is the sky's vec4 for the fog and the two must not share a name (a redefinition made the water vanish in both regions, and only a browser shows it).
- Audio in the Fen: `AudioState.fen` turns the gulls off, lowers the lapping and brings up `fen-day` and `fen-night`, which cross over at dusk and dawn. The three road chimes are picked in order (`sfx(..., { pick })`) so a row of lights plays a rising tune.
- The simulated keeper (`tools/sim/player.ts`) crosses to the Fen for jobs and for better fishing, takes her pots up first, and after the story fills the journal. `tests/sim.test.ts` holds the Fen's bands.

## The crew and the boats, for whoever touches them next
- The rules are `core/crew.ts` and nothing else earns for the crew. `tickCrew(state, dt, env, kit)` runs from `Hands.step`, which runs in the fixed step: no offline earnings is a fixed point of the brief. A hand's clock stands still under way (|speed| 0.5 or more, or the boat busy), while it is too light for them (`when: "dark"`: night, or fog of 0.3 and over), and while the basket is full. They land only journal fish of the zone under the keel (`crewTable`), with no traits and no journal credit.
- Who is aboard is worked out, never stored: each hand names a berth in `crew.json` (`berth`: the cat 0, her perch; Mister Hollis 1, the bench) and `aboard(state)` is the hired hands whose berth the current boat has; the rest are `ashore`. Nobody takes anybody else's berth, so the skiff (one berth) never carries Mister Hollis. Once hired, a hand is never shown where they used to wait (`signedOn`). The basket (`state.crew.hold`) belongs to the keeper, not to a boat: its size is the current boat's `crewHold`, and a basket fuller than a smaller boat's size simply stops the hands until it is sold.
- Boats are data (`boats.json`: `crew` berths, `crewHold`, `price`, `needs` a flag to be offered, `sets` a flag when bought). `core/gear.ts buyBoat` and `takeBoat` are the whole of buying and swapping as far as the save goes; `Town.buyBoat` is the fade and the next morning around it, and `Sailing.fitOut` changes the hull in the scene (the hands step off first: `Hands.unship`). The Bell keeps boats and crew, sells the basket with the hold, and sets the hands' clocks back to nothing (`turnCrew`).
- A quest objective `own` counts a boat; `berth` is met when the current boat has the berth of the hand the job signs on. A reward `crew: [id]` signs a hand on. A place can be `fromBoat` (read from the helm once she has all but stopped) and can stand only `until` a flag (the wreck, until `puffin-afloat`); a region's `hails` are people who stand on the water and are spoken to from the boat, within `r` of the hull.
- A model boat brings its berths as nodes `Crew1`, `Crew2` (origin on what the hand sits or stands on; the node's own turn is the way they face). `BoatRig.berth(i)` is the group to add a figure to. The skiff's one berth and the Puffin stand-in's two are numbers in `boat-rig.ts`.
- The boat's well is kept dry by a lid that writes depth only (`asHullMask`, render order 4, before the sea at 5). Anything blended that sits below that lid is cut off by it. That is why Mister Hollis is thinned by dither in the solid pass (`crew-figures.ts THIN`) and not by opacity, and why the fog of his face, which is blended, is drawn after the sea (render order 5.5).
- The figures aboard are `crew-figures.ts`: `CatAboard` (the sitting sculpt with `watch` and `eat`, the standing one for `swat`) and `HollisAboard` (`sit`, `pull`, the node `LineHand`). Each still works without its clips or its model. A catch is shown by `Hands.landed`: the cat's fish is flicked aboard, Hollis's hangs from his fist until 1.72 s into `pull`.
- The wreck sits on the bed (`fen-props.ts`: `aground` is the water over her origin) with the reeds kept `clearing` metres back. The only open water beside her is to the north-west: bots sail to (-1034, -1001).
- `e2e/crew.cjs` creeps the last metres to Mister Hollis (`window.crew.creep`): the Puffin carries her way, and the hail needs the hull within 9 m and all but stopped.
- Scratch scripts that skip rendering (`g.skipRender`) must still call `g.hands.present(dt)` if they mean to look at a catch afterwards: the flick and the swat are advanced there.

## Things fished up, for whoever touches them next
- What a cast meets is looked at once, at the splash, by `peekFind` (`core/finds.ts`), and the dice go by the save's seed and `finds.casts`. The count moves only in `countCast`, which the session calls through `ctx.spent` when a fish is hooked, the find is aboard, or a crate or chest goes with the line. Keep the two apart: counting at the splash is what let a keeper cast and reel in to farm finds (the v0.6.0 review). Always make all three throws in `peekFind`, or one water's dice move another's. `tests/finds.test.ts` holds all of it. A forced find (`Game.forcedFind`) does not count a cast.
- A find is never a tax: a crate carries `worth` (`fishWorth`: what a fish of this cast would fetch a good hand) and pays at least that. If fishing income changes shape (a new multiplier outside `stats()`, a new bonus for a clean cast or a perfect hook), put it in `fishWorth` or `typicalFish(…, best)`: the test that lands six hundred fish in each of three waters will say if it has been left out.
- Nothing is told to the keeper while `Game.behindTitle` is set. Anything new that is said on a clock (not in answer to something the player did) should wait for it too.
- A new relic is a row in `relics.json`: `where` (zones, hours, a need) puts it in the water; without `where` it is a gift and some job or page must give it (the content tests fail a relic nothing gives). `hint.after` is when its rumour is told. A new kind of need goes in `relics.ts met`. Its held-up shape is a function in `find-models.ts RELIC_SHAPES`; without one it is shown as the oilcloth bundle.
- A new region needs a `crate` and a `chest` in `finds.json` (`validateFinds` says so), with chest zones that exist there.
- The haul is `FishingSession.updateHaul`. It has no stamina and no fish: `session.fight` is null throughout, so anything that reads the fight must read `session.onLine` or check the phase. Every bot's fishing policy has a line for it: reel unless it is snagged, has knocked, or the tension is over 0.7.
- Held models face the character's left at identity (the catch socket's +Z), so `find-models.ts` turns them by `HELD_YAW`. The sizes in `SIZE` were set by eye against the keeper in screenshots.
- Pearls are set, never spent: `state.moonpearls` is how many the keeper has, `state.pearls[relic]` how many stand beside a relic, and `pearlsFree` the difference. Anything that takes a pearl away for good must lift set ones first.
- Scratch scripts: set `g.skipRender = true` while stepping and `false` only for a screenshot, or every step draws in the software renderer.

## Gear, pots and the Bell, for whoever touches them next
- Add a bonus by adding an effect to data (`gear.json`, `echoes.json`, `relics.json`) and, if it is a new kind, one line in `core/gear.ts stats()`. Do not add bonuses anywhere else: the Gear page and the simulator read `stats()`.
- After any tuning change run `npm run sim` and `npx vitest run tests/sim.test.ts`. The tests are the tuning targets; change a target on purpose or not at all.
- Pots tick in `Game.step` through `PotField.step` and only there. Nothing may earn outside the fixed step: no offline earnings is a fixed point of the brief.
- A pot lands further out the higher the keeper stands (`PotField.findSpot`), so it shows past a dock's edge. The prompt sits low while a pot is in reach so it does not cover the float. A press of F hauls or sets; an empty pot is taken up by holding F (`TAKE_UP_HOLD`).
- A fishing session keeps the gear it was cast with. `Game.refreshSession()` rebuilds it at once when the rod is idle and otherwise marks it stale, and the step rebuilds it the moment the line is in. The hook spends `session.ctx.bait.id`, not whatever is on the hook now.
- The v0.3.0 reviewer's proof scripts showed how to test this slice without a browser: small `tsx` scripts against `src/core` and `src/game/talk.ts`.
- `ringBell(state)` in `core/bell.ts` is the whole Turning as far as the save goes; `Town.ringBell()` is the cutscene around it and applies the quest event without a toast until the fade lifts. A new thing the tide should take or keep goes in the core function and in `tests/gear.test.ts`.
- The belfry model leans toward its +X and its bell swings about Z. `world-scene.ts` turns it so its +Z opening faces the rope post, leads the rope from the clapper (`RopeSocket`) over that sill, and re-lays the inside length every frame while the bell moves.
- Bots that play this slice use `window.tide` helpers defined at the top of `e2e/tide.cjs`.
- Two traps for bots, both met while finishing this slice. The game saves as the page leaves (`beforeunload`), so a bot that plants a save in `localStorage` must stop `game.save` first or its own game lands on top. And a job handed in can run straight on into the giver's next offer, where there is no goodbye to pick: turn the offer down first (`e2e/sail.cjs` after the flounder).
